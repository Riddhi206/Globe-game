                        How to play

                        1. Guess any country. It lights up on the globe with a colour showing how close you are.
                        2. Follow the colour. Blue = far away, shades through violet and crimson to dark red = very close.
                        3. Use the arrows. Each guess points toward the answer.
                        4. Stuck? From your 5th guess, tap 🎁 Win a hint for a quick quiz. Get it right for a hint about the mystery country.
                        5. Stuck? Use a 🔭 spyglass to see every country’s name. Earn one per 5-day streak (max 3).
                        6. Points = your rank. Fewer guesses always wins (found in 3 = 800+). Ties go to the sharper aim: how close your earlier guesses landed.

                        Modes

                        • Daily — one country for everyone, 10 guesses. New one every day.
                        • Atlas — the 🗺️ on the home screen: spin the globe with every country named.
                        • Classic — a random country, as many guesses as you need. Your best 10 finds each week count on the weekly Classic leaderboard.
                        • The Unmaking — story mode. Seven chapters, seven Shadows, one choice.
